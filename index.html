About St Gandalfs
An engineering workshop for biomedical technology.

St Gandalfs is an open biomedical engineering initiative originating from Biomechanics+ at the University of York.

Rather than keeping useful engineering tools inside individual research projects, we aim to turn them into things that others can inspect, reproduce, adapt, teach with and improve.

The projects span biomedical engineering rather than a single research discipline — from electronics and electrophysiology to movement tracking, software, sensing and beyond.

Part of Biomechanics+ · University of York
St Gandalfs is a project-focused open engineering initiative. Research outputs and academic information remain available through the relevant University and research-group websites.
